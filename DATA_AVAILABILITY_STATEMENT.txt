The source code, trained TD3 and DDPG actor models, processed numerical results, random-seed statistics, observation-ablation results, integration-step sensitivity results, and regenerated manuscript figures supporting the revised study are provided in this repository. The revised experiments use a 500-us controller update period and RK4 integration with a 50-us internal step, with additional 25-us and 10-us numerical-sensitivity checks. TD3 and DDPG are independently trained under the same six random seeds (0-5) using matched state, reward, action, network, and training settings. The repository is intended to support reproduction of the simulation-based results; it does not provide hardware/HIL validation data.
